# Project Statement: Campus Balance

## Problem Statement

College students often have to balance their studies with rest, health, social activities, finances, and screen time. Choices that help in one area may affect another. For example, studying can improve academic performance but also increase stress and reduce energy.

Campus Balance is a text-based simulation that lets users make these kinds of choices and observe how they affect a student’s life over time.

## Scope of the Project

Campus Balance simulates one student’s college life for seven days. The player has 12 activity hours available each day and can choose from activities such as attending classes, studying, sleeping, exercising, socializing, using a phone, and working part-time.

Activities and random events affect the student’s money, energy, health, stress, attendance, academic score, happiness, screen time, and sleep. The program displays warnings when some statistics reach concerning levels. At the end of the simulation, it generates a weekly summary and saves it to `reports/weekly_report.txt`.

The project is a simplified educational simulation. It does not use real student data or provide personalized academic, medical, or financial advice.

## Target Users

- Students interested in how daily choices can affect different areas of college life.
- Beginners learning Python programming.
- Educators looking for a small example of a menu-driven simulation project.

## High-Level Features

- Simulate student life over a seven-day week.
- Choose from seven activities with different time and statistic effects.
- Manage daily time and energy.
- Track money, health, stress, attendance, academic score, happiness, screen time, and sleep.
- Respond to random events with different choices and outcomes.
- View warnings when certain student statistics become concerning.
- Generate a weekly report showing final statistics and major problem areas.
- Save the report to `reports/weekly_report.txt`.
